# Playtest checklist — 2026-08-18

Everything below landed today and is **harness-verified but never played**. Ordered by how
likely it is to be wrong in a way only playing reveals.

⚠️ **Restart the editor completely before playing.** Many scripts, scenes and `.tres` files
were edited externally; reloading a tab is not enough.

## 1. Tune-me-now (numbers I guessed, one constant each)
- **Charge pulse on the big die** — it was invisible for a month (its animation had been
  hollowed out). It's back as a brightness+reach flare. *Is it too shy, or too loud?*
  → `dice.gd`, the flare block in `_on_dice_charged`.
- **Surprise roll flights** — ~2% of big rolls arrive as a different animation (including
  the spinning ones), max 2 per fight. *Too rare to notice? Too often?*
  → `SURPRISE_ROLL_*` constants at the top of `dice.gd`.
- **Block ward** — new; player's is tinted by the active dice type, enemies' always icy blue.
- **Magma burn** — new; a hot bloom + embers on every enemy hit. Had zero VFX before.

## 2. Things that should now be RIGHT (confirm, don't assume)
- **Map dice badge**: buy the **deal die in the card shop**, then look at map room badges.
  They used to claim "affordable" using a price from before that purchase. This is the one
  fix the harness could not simulate.
- **Flux tooltip** now says "one roll per Power chain" — which is what the code always did.
  The old text ("same Dice type twice in a row") was simply false.
- **Top-bar dice**: hover each counter → tooltip with name, faces, infusion.
- **Event text** is Belwe now (matches the buttons). Five events used to overflow and clip.
- **Campfire + deck view buttons** on the painted plates.

## 3. Enemy ramp (Wave A) — feel, not correctness
Bigger Satyr, Bigger Kraken and Goblin now gain **+1 Strength** on one specific beat, shown
by a second icon on that intent. Maelstrom 12→13, Lava Hound 6→7 per hit, Goblin 7→9.
- Do the fights now have a clock, or do they just feel longer?
- Does the rider icon read, or is it noise?

## 4. New art — 19 cards + relic + icons
Draft them and look at them **in hand**, at card size. Two icons I already flagged as weak
were regenerated (Second Socket, Artillery).

## 5. Sound auditions (needs your files)
Drop candidates in `debug_sfx_candidates\crush\` and `\riser\`, then in any fight:
**F9** cycles the big-roll crush, **F10** cycles the riser (fires at the max-roll hang,
leading into the crush). Each press previews it and prints the filename; cycling wraps back
through the current default so you can always A/B.

## 5b. Font pass (2026-08-19) — look, don't hunt
Every screen now speaks one of five faces: **Cinzel Decorative** = screen titles,
**Cinzel** = card chrome, **Belwe** = body text + every button, **LuckiestGuy** = every HUD
number, **MinionPro** = card descriptions + banners. Tooltip *bodies* stay NotoSans.
- **Tutorial** is the one place with real fit risk — Belwe is 22% wider than the font it
  replaced. Rendered every step (worst case 3 lines, fits), but read them in game.
- Deliberately unchanged: End Turn, ROLL, the Power number, card faces, HP bars.
- Also fixed while in there: the card bonus-requirement chip had **no font at all**, and
  `[b]` inside tooltips/card text silently rendered in the wrong face.
- 5.4 MB of never-referenced fonts left the project (archived, not deleted).

## 6. Known-and-deliberate (not bugs)
- Medusa still applies **Weak**, not Depleted — Depleted has no mechanical effect yet, so
  applying it would show a badge that does nothing. Needs a decision.
- Lava Hound has **no Molten Roar** — it needs the first HP-percentage check in the codebase.
- Dice shop + tutorial overlay buttons deliberately left on their own styles.
